# Re:TUI Keys Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Re:TUI Keys is a keyboard that works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything you type, say or copy to anyone, including its developer.

## What Keys does not do

- It does not connect to the internet.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- It does not upload, sell or share your data.
- It is excluded from Android's cloud backup.

## What stays on your device

Keys stores the following in its own private app storage. Other apps cannot read it, and it is deleted when you uninstall Keys or clear its data.

- Settings and theme.
- Words you add to the dictionary and words Keys learns as you type, including which words tend to follow each other, corrections you make repeatedly, and small touch adjustments for each key.
- Your usual speaking level, a single number used to ignore background voices during on-device voice typing.
- Clipboard history, only if you turn on clipboard saving. Entries expire after the number of days you choose.
- Glide typing diagnostics, only if you turn that option on.
- Language packs and voice packs you import.

You can view and edit learned words and corrections under Settings, Dictionary. Suggestions and learning can be turned off in settings.

## Microphone

Keys asks for the microphone only for voice typing, and listens only while the mic key is active.

- With an installed voice pack, speech is turned into text on the device. Audio is not saved and never leaves the device.
- If you choose "System default" or another speech app as the voice engine, that app records and processes your speech under its own privacy policy. Keys only receives the resulting text.

## Backups

"Backup keyboard profile" writes a file to a location you pick. It contains your settings, dictionary and learned corrections. Keys does not send it anywhere; what happens to the file afterwards is up to you.

## Other apps

If the Re:TUI launcher is installed, the two apps exchange theme and shortcut information on the device. Nothing you type is shared with it.

## Children

Keys does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
